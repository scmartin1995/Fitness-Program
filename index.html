Progression Rules
Primary Lifts
Hit top reps → +2.5–5 lb next session. Miss reps → hold load.
AR-7 Blocks
Reduce rest first → add reps → small load increase every 2–3 weeks.
Micro Arms (Push/Pull)
Add reps first, then load slowly. Stop 1–2 reps from failure.
Close-Grip Bench
Same logic as Bench and OHP — hit top reps, earn the load.
EZ-Bar Curl (Sunday)
Add reps first (6→8), then +2.5 lb.
Bulgarian Split Squat
Add reps before load. Keep it submaximal — legs are pre-fatigued.
Single-Leg RDL
Do not aggressively progress. Pattern quality is the goal. Add a 3-sec pause at the bottom before adding load.
Hip Thrust
+5 lb every 2 weeks if form is clean through full ROM.
Landmine Rotations
Train speed and pattern first — load increases only every 3–4 weeks.
Farmer Carries
Increase distance before load.
Recovery Guardrails
— Deload every 6–8 weeks
— Eat enough carbs
— Sleep is mandatory
— Never AR-7: deadlifts, heavy squats, Olympic lifts
Golf Notes
— Landmine + cable rotations are pattern training — ego stays outside
— Hip mobility unlocks rotational power you already have
— Farmer carries = grip training + spinal decompression
— Pallof Press builds anti-rotation stability that holds under fatigue
