description

Program Philosophy

A strength and hypertrophy program inspired by the AR-7 method. The canonical protocol uses descending pyramids of 7×7, 6×6, and 5×5 reps with only 7-second rest intervals at ~65% 1RM — a high-density metabolic approach designed to maximize time under tension.

repeatFrequency

4 mandatory lifting sessions per week (Thu–Sun). Short rest, paired sets, sustained metabolic stress.

trending_downDeload

Every 6–8 weeks. Reduce volume, maintain intensity. Sleep and nutrition are non-negotiable.

Never AR-7: deadlifts, heavy squats, or Olympic lifts. Quality of movement over absolute load.

Golf Adaptation

Rotational Power

Landmine rotations and cable work train the hip-to-shoulder sequence as a pattern. Load is secondary to speed and rotation quality.

Hip Mobility

90/90 switches and hip CARs are programmed as warm-up work on leg days. Range of motion is a direct multiplier on clubhead speed.

Grip & Decompression

Farmer carries and dead hangs close out pull and arm days. Grip endurance holds up in the back nine.

Anti-Rotation

Pallof Press trains the ability to resist rotation under load — the same stability that keeps your swing on plane when fatigued.

Primary Lifts

Hit top reps → +2.5–5 lb next session. Miss reps → hold load.

AR-7 Blocks

Reduce rest first → add reps → small load increase every 2–3 weeks.

Recovery Guardrails

— Deload every 6–8 weeks

— Eat enough carbs

— Sleep is mandatory

— Never AR-7: deadlifts, heavy squats, Olympic lifts
